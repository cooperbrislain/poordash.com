# PoorDash

**Earn Chitcoin. Eat Local.™**

A satirical landing page for a dystopian gig-economy app — *Dope Wars* if the drugs were delivery gigs and the loan shark was a venture-backed startup with a five-star rating.

Live at [poordash.com](https://poordash.com) (someone else's, not mine — hence the project).

---

## What this is

PoorDash started as a domain I registered when the gig economy was new and the whole thing felt like an obvious joke. I sat on it for over a decade.

The problem with satire is that reality doesn't wait. By the time I got around to building anything, the dystopian version I'd imagined had become a pitch deck, then a unicorn, then just… Tuesday. Hard to parody something that's already parodying itself.

Now, thanks to AI, I can at least keep up.

The premise: contractors earn **Chitcoin (CHIT)** — a proprietary token worth 4× more at participating merchants than on the open BTC market, specifically engineered to disincentivize cashing out. CHIT → USD withdrawal is pending regulatory approval.

---

## Built with AI

This project was built using [Claude](https://claude.ai/claude-code) as the primary development tool. I'm not presenting it as a showcase of hand-coded frontend skills — it's a showcase of what's possible when you have a clear creative vision and the right AI tool to execute it.

The concept, copy, design direction, and product decisions are mine. The implementation is Claude's. That distinction feels worth being honest about.

---

## Stack

Single `index.html`. No framework, no build step, no dependencies beyond two Google Fonts. Drops onto any static host.
