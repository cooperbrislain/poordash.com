Getting Started

Three Easy Steps to Financial Independence

We've streamlined onboarding so you can start accumulating CHIT as quickly as possible.

1
Sign Up

Create your Contractor Account in minutes. We'll need your name, address (if applicable), Social Security number, driver's license, vehicle information, bank details, and acknowledgment of our 52-page Independent Contractor Agreement. You'll also need to consent to CHIT Wallet Terms separately.

2
Accept Gigs

Browse available orders and accept the ones that work for you. We recommend maintaining an acceptance rate above 70% to remain eligible for the platform. This is not a requirement. It's just a recommendation. Please maintain it.

3
Get Paid in CHIT

Each completed delivery earns cash (deposited weekly, minus applicable fees) plus a CHIT bonus, automatically credited to your PoorDash Wallet. CHIT can then be redeemed at participating merchants for discounts. CHIT cannot be converted back to cash at this time.

Why Chitcoin?

Traditional wages are limited by mathematics. CHIT is not. While we cannot guarantee increases to your base delivery rate, there is no ceiling on the CHIT bonuses we can offer — and no floor either, to be transparent about it.

Think of CHIT as a stake in the PoorDash ecosystem — a way to participate in the value you help create. It is not a stake in PoorDash. It does not confer ownership, voting rights, or claims on future earnings. But it does get you 20% off a burrito at FuelMart™.

CHIT is not a security. CHIT is not a currency. CHIT is not an investment vehicle or a promise of future value. CHIT is Chitcoin. CHIT is yours.

Contractor Benefits

Everything You Need.
Nothing You Don't.

PoorDash gives contractors the tools, flexibility, and digital currency to succeed in the modern economy. Benefits traditionally associated with employment are available separately, at your expense.

🕐
Your Schedule

Work whenever you want. We'll notify you of available orders during windows when demand justifies deployment. You are not required to be available. We are not required to have orders for you. This is what flexibility looks like.

Your Business

You're not an employee — you're an entrepreneur. That means freedom, autonomy, and the opportunity to deduct mileage on your taxes. It also means you buy your own gas, maintain your own vehicle, source your own insurance, and file quarterly estimated taxes.

⭐
Your Rating

Customers rate every delivery on a 5-star scale. Maintain a 4.5 average or above to remain eligible for the platform. Ratings below this threshold trigger automated account review. The algorithm is objective, consistent, and cannot be appealed to directly.

🪙
Your CHIT

Every delivery earns Chitcoin. CHIT builds up in your wallet and can be redeemed at participating businesses — turning your surplus labor value into purchasing power within the PoorDash ecosystem. It's not a raise. It's better than a raise. It's CHIT.

Featured Program
Experiencing a Housing Transition?

We've got you covered. PoorDash's Vehicle-Based Contractor Program offers flexible earning options specifically designed for dashers in transitional living situations — including those whose vehicle serves as their primary residence. No fixed address required. Just a smartphone, a tank of gas, and the drive to succeed.

No lease. No long-term commitment. No guarantee of availability. Because we know you value flexibility in all areas of your life.
